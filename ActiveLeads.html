Setup & Configuration
Follow these instructions to connect each data source. Once connected in Cowork, leads will pull live data automatically.
🔵

ZoomInfo

Connected via Cowork MCP

ZoomInfo is your primary source for company signals, contact data, intent scores, scoops, and funding events. Make sure your ZoomInfo MCP is connected in Cowork settings.

1
In Cowork → Settings → Connectors, verify ZoomInfo is connected. You should see a green "Connected" badge.
2
Enable Intent Signals in your ZoomInfo plan. This is required for the "Unfilled Jobs 30d+" and "ERP/WMS Hiring" signals.
3
Enable Scoops — this surfaces leadership changes (new IT/Ops Directors) and expansion news automatically.
4
Set your company filter to: Industry → [your selected industries], Employees → 25–5,000, Revenue → $10M–$500M, Location → [your city] +radius.
KEY SEARCH FILTERS TO USE
Company Filter: industries: logistics, manufacturing, retail, hospitality employees: 25–5000 revenue: $10M–$500M hq_radius: 50mi from Waltham MA Intent Topics: "IT Staffing", "ERP Implementation", "WMS Software", "Workday HR" Scoops: "new hire", "expanding", "implementing"
🟠

Apify

Web Scraping for Job Signals

Apify scrapes LinkedIn job postings and company news to find companies actively hiring IT/ops roles — the strongest "ready to engage" signal you can find.

1
Connect Apify in Cowork → Settings → Connectors. You'll need your Apify API Token from apify.com/account/integrations.
2
Use the LinkedIn Jobs Scraper actor (Actor ID: curious_coder/linkedin-jobs-scraper). This pulls open roles by keyword + location.
3
Use the Google News Scraper actor to catch ERP/WMS implementation announcements or company expansions not on LinkedIn.
4
Schedule both scrapers to run every Monday morning so your pipeline is fresh at the start of each outreach week.
LINKEDIN JOBS ACTOR — INPUT CONFIG
{ "queries": [ "IT Director logistics Boston", "ERP implementation manager MA", "WMS analyst warehouse New England", "Director of Engineering manufacturing" ], "location": "Greater Boston Area", "datePosted": "past month", "maxResults": 100 }
📊

Google Sheets

CRM & Pipeline Tracker

Your Google Sheet acts as the live CRM. Every lead found here can be exported with one click — including score, signals, contacts, and recommended outreach touch.

1
Connect Google Sheets in Cowork → Settings → Connectors using your Google account.
2
Create a new Sheet titled "Active Leads Pipeline". The export button will write to this sheet automatically.
3
Required columns (auto-populated on export):
Column A: Company Name Column B: Score (1–10) Column C: Temperature (Hot/Warm/Cold) Column D: Industry Column E: Employee Count Column F: Key Signals Column G: Primary Contact Column H: Contact Title Column I: LinkedIn URL Column J: Data Source (ZI/Apify/Both) Column K: Date Found Column L: Touch Status (New/Contacted/Reply) Column M: Notes

Claude AI Scoring

Automatic via Cowork

Claude reads the raw signals from ZoomInfo and Apify and scores each company 1–10 on their likelihood of being ready to engage a recruiting agency. No setup needed — it runs automatically.

SCORING CRITERIA (WHAT CLAUDE CHECKS)
+3
Unfilled IT/ops roles 30+ days — they're already failing to hire on their own
+2
New IT/Ops Director hired in last 90 days — new leaders build new teams fast
+2
ERP/WMS/Workday implementation news — they need technical talent NOW
+1
Headcount growth 10%+ in 6 months — scaling fast, recruiting strain
+1
Recent funding or expansion — budget unlocked for hiring
+1
ZoomInfo Intent score high on staffing topics — researching solutions
